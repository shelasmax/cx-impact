> The host emails the visitor's name, visit date, and host team to the reception inbox by 15:00 on the preceding business day. A receptionist copies the request into the visitor log. The security desk checks the visit date against the restricted-date list. If the date is clear, reception emails an approval to the host. If the date is restricted, reception emails that the visit is not approved and asks the host to choose another date. On arrival, the visitor shows photo identification at the north reception desk. The receptionist matches the visitor to an approved log entry. If the identification matches, the visitor signs the paper register and receives a numbered badge. If it does not match, the receptionist does not issue a badge and calls the host. The visitor returns the badge to the north reception desk before leaving, and the receptionist marks it returned in the visitor log.
